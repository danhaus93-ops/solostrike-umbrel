# SoloStrike Trademark Policy

**Effective: April 2026**

This document describes how the **SoloStrike** name, logo, icon, and visual identity may and may not be used.

---

## What's covered

The following are **trademarks of SoloStrike** (the project, owned by the SoloStrike maintainer, GitHub handle `danhaus93-ops`):

- The name **"SoloStrike"** (and stylized variants like "SOLOSTRIKE")
- The **pickaxe-and-Bitcoin-glyph icon** in all its variants (the rounded-square app tile, the transparent silhouette, splash screens, favicons)
- The **LAVA visual identity**: metallic-gold gradient hashrate numbers, the amber/gold/dark color palette, the SoloStrike-branded card styling
- Brand vocabulary that originated with this project: **"Strikes"**, **"Strikers"**, **"The Hunt"**, **"The Vein"**, **"The Burn"**, **"The Reckoning"**, **"Claim Jumpers"**, **"Hot Streak"**, **"The Ledger"**, **"Firepower"**, **"The Crew"**, **"Solostrike Pulse"**, **"Strike Mesh"**, **"Solo Strike Map"**, **"Pool Pin"**, **"Hash Ticker"**, **"Near Strikes"**, **"Top Diggers"**
- The coinbase tag string **"/SoloStrike on Umbrel/"**

These marks are distinct from, and not covered by, the AGPL-3.0 license that applies to the source code. Trademark rights persist even when source code is freely licensed — this is standard practice for projects like Docker, Mozilla Firefox, WordPress, and Linux.

## What you CAN do without permission

- ✅ **Use SoloStrike** for any purpose (personal, commercial, hosted) under the AGPL-3.0 license.
- ✅ **Modify the source code** under the AGPL-3.0 license, including the right to fork the project.
- ✅ **Refer to SoloStrike by name** in factual contexts: documentation, blog posts, tutorials, reviews, comparisons, mining setup guides ("I use SoloStrike on my Umbrel"), bug reports, support discussions.
- ✅ **Link to the official SoloStrike repository** or website using the SoloStrike name.
- ✅ **Use unmodified SoloStrike screenshots** in articles, reviews, and tutorials that discuss the project.
- ✅ **Distribute unmodified SoloStrike** under its actual name and branding (the standard AGPL distribution case).
- ✅ **Contribute** to this project via pull requests, issues, and discussions.

## What you CANNOT do without written permission

- ❌ **Use "SoloStrike" or confusingly similar names** ("SoloStrikr", "SoloStrike Pro", "Strike Solo", "Solo-Strike") for your own competing mining application, service, or commercial product.
- ❌ **Use the SoloStrike pickaxe icon, logo, or visual identity** in your own product, mining service, or commercial venture.
- ❌ **Brand a fork of SoloStrike as "SoloStrike"** if you have made substantive modifications. Forks must use a clearly distinct name and identity to avoid user confusion.
- ❌ **Sell goods or services under the SoloStrike name** (shirts, stickers, hosted mining services, paid support, consulting branded as "SoloStrike Support") without permission.
- ❌ **Imply official endorsement** — for example, claiming your product is "by SoloStrike", "approved by SoloStrike", "the official SoloStrike service", or "SoloStrike Premium" without an actual agreement in place.
- ❌ **Register the SoloStrike name or confusingly similar names** as a trademark, domain name, social media handle, or business name with the intent to compete with or misrepresent the project.
- ❌ **Use the brand vocabulary** ("Strikers", "The Hunt", "Pulse", "Claim Jumpers", etc.) as the primary identity of a competing product. Factual reference within your product's documentation when discussing interoperability with SoloStrike is fine.

## Forking guidance

If you fork SoloStrike under AGPL-3.0 and intend to operate the fork as a separate project:

1. **Pick a new name.** Not "SoloStrike-Plus", not "SoloStrike Community Edition", not "SoloStrike Fork". A genuinely distinct name like "PickPool", "MyMiner", "BlockHunt", whatever — make it clearly your own.
2. **Replace the visual identity.** Use your own icon, your own color palette, your own card chrome.
3. **Change the coinbase tag.** Don't ship your fork tagged `/SoloStrike on Umbrel/`. Ship it tagged `/<YourName> on Umbrel/` — this is both a trademark concern and basic respect.
4. **Reference SoloStrike factually** if you want to credit the origin: "based on SoloStrike (AGPL-3.0)" or "originally forked from SoloStrike". This is allowed and encouraged.

The AGPL-3.0 license guarantees your right to fork the code. This trademark policy says: if you do, build your own brand on top of the freedom the license gives you.

## Why this exists

Open-source licenses protect the **code**. Trademark policies protect **trust**.

When users see "SoloStrike," they should know they're getting:
- The actual SoloStrike source code (verifiable on the official repository)
- The actual SoloStrike maintainer's release decisions
- The privacy and security properties advertised by the project
- The Pulse network protocol that interoperates with other real SoloStrike installs

A trademark policy makes that promise enforceable. If someone ships a malicious lookalike called "SoloStrike" that steals Bitcoin addresses or breaks the Pulse privacy model, this policy gives the project standing to stop them.

It's not about controlling the code — the AGPL-3.0 keeps the code free. It's about keeping the **name and identity** trustworthy.

## Permission requests

Want to use the SoloStrike name or branding in a way this policy doesn't permit? Open an issue at:

**https://github.com/danhaus93-ops/solostrike-umbrel/issues**

Title it `[trademark]` and describe what you'd like to do. Reasonable commercial-context requests (writing a book that includes SoloStrike screenshots, hosting a meetup, building integrations that need to display the SoloStrike name and logo) will usually be approved with simple ground rules. Bad-faith requests (squatting the name, lookalike apps, paid services trading on the brand) will not.
